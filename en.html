About Us

Together We Grow: Empowering Singapore-China Relations.


China-Singapore Training Centre Pte. Ltd. (Channel No.: 991909), based in Singapore, is a premier organization with over 20 years of expertise in training and consulting. Recognized by China’s State Administration of Foreign Experts Affairs, our institute offers a wide range of administrative and corporate management courses.


With strong support from both nations’ governments, universities, and business communities, we have become a trusted bridge fostering economic and cultural ties between China and Singapore. Our services include professional training, international conferences, management consulting, and project promotions, leveraging Singapore’s successful practices to provide cutting-edge solutions tailored to China’s unique needs.

Services

Customized Training for Local Government Excellence

We bring Singaporean experts, retired senior officials, and scholars to China to provide professional consulting and training, enhancing government excellence.


Our programs address budget and time constraints, offering expert-led courses in administration, investment, and asset management.

Insightful Training Courses Through Singapore Partnerships

We deliver high-quality training courses in Singapore with the help of Singaporean government agencies, including the EDB, CPIB, and HDB, as well as business associations and leading enterprises.


Our programs span hundreds of study tours and exchanges, featuring seminars, field visits, discussions, and internships.

Business Study Trip

We connect Chinese business leaders with Singapore’s top experts, senior officials, and industry pioneers, offering deep insights into governance, innovation, and investment opportunities.


Our programs combine expert-led sessions with exclusive site visits, tailored to maximize impact while respecting budget and time constraints.

Consultations & Workshops

We provide tailored consultations and workshops designed to address specific challenges in administration, corporate management, and policy implementation.


We focus on actionable solutions in areas like strategic planning, governance reforms, and sustainable development, ensuring immediate relevance and impact for clients.

Government Liaison Services

Our team specializes in facilitating connections with Singaporean government agencies, fostering collaboration on joint initiatives and knowledge sharing.


We assist with networking, project coordination, and communication to enable clients to access Singapore’s world-class expertise in urban development, technology, and public administration.

Summer Camp

Our immersive summer camp programs combine education, cultural exploration, and leadership development for young participants.


Our camps feature interactive workshops, hands-on activities, and site visits to iconic institutions, fostering global perspectives and nurturing the next generation of innovative thinkers and leaders.
